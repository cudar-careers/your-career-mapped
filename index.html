      Four ways to develop

      Choose the level of exposure or movement that fits what you need.

      Punting, Walking, Cycling and Train describe the type and intensity of development, not seniority. Use one mode, combine several or move directly when your experience already aligns.

      A practical approach to career mobility

      Career paths do not have to be linear.

      The capabilities you build in one function can create opportunities in another, opening up new ways to grow, broaden your experience and shape what comes next.
