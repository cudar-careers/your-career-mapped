      04 · Skills bank

      SB
      Definition

      What is a Skills Bank?

      The Skills Bank is a shared language for understanding the capabilities, knowledge and experience that exist across the organisation. It helps employees recognise skills they already use, understand how those capabilities translate across functions, and identify specialist knowledge they may want to build for future career moves.

      05 · Career journeys in practice

      Real routes. Real careers.

      These anonymised journeys show why cross-functional movement matters: a sideways move can be a bridge, an accelerator, or a way to return stronger.

      A practical approach to career mobility

      Career paths do not have to be linear.

      The capabilities you build in one function can create opportunities in another, opening up new ways to grow, broaden your experience and shape what comes next — across CUDAR and throughout your career.
